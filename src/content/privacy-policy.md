## 1. Who We Are

Get A Job is a career guidance platform that helps job seekers — primarily students and early-career professionals in Israel — build their career through AI-powered tools. These include career analysis, CV generation, job suggestions, LinkedIn content optimization, application tracking, and personalized coaching.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have regarding your data. It applies to all users of the Get A Job platform at getajob.careers and to the Get A Job browser extension.

We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, as amended by Amendment No. 13 (effective August 14, 2025), and the regulations enacted thereunder.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

### Data you provide directly

- **Identity and contact information:** Full name, email address, phone number
- **Education history:** Institutions attended, degrees, fields of study, graduation dates
- **Work experience:** Job titles, employers, employment dates, descriptions of responsibilities and achievements, employment type (full-time, part-time, volunteering, military service)
- **Skills and qualifications:** Technical skills, languages, certifications
- **Career goals:** Desired roles, target industries, preferred work environments, career direction, five-year goals
- **CV/resume content:** The full text and data extracted from CVs you upload
- **LinkedIn archive data:** If you choose to upload your LinkedIn data export, we parse profile information, positions, education, skills, honors, volunteering, languages, and job seeker preferences. We do **not** store your LinkedIn connections data.
- **Stories and experiences:** Structured narratives about your professional accomplishments that you create or confirm through the Story Bank feature
- **Application tracking data:** Jobs you apply to, application statuses, outcomes, and notes
- **Job descriptions:** Job posting text you paste into the platform or the browser extension to generate a tailored CV and match
- **Chat conversations:** Your messages with our AI career agents (Career, CV, Interview, and Skill Advisor agents)
- **Survey responses:** Answers to onboarding and feedback surveys, including how you heard about us
- **Constraints and preferences:** Work environment preferences, availability constraints

### Data we generate about you

- **Career analysis results:** AI-generated tier classifications, role recommendations, skill gap analysis, and qualification assessments
- **Tailored CVs:** AI-generated versions of your CV, customized for specific job descriptions
- **LinkedIn content:** AI-generated profile optimizations, post drafts, comment suggestions, and outreach messages
- **Job match scores:** Automated scoring of job listings against your profile
- **Daily action recommendations:** Personalized task suggestions based on your career goals and activity
- **Learning path recommendations:** Suggested courses and resources based on identified skill gaps

### Data collected automatically

- **Authentication data:** Login timestamps, session tokens
- **Device and browser data:** IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen resolution
- **Usage data:** Pages visited, time spent on pages, features used, actions taken within the platform (this data is associated with your account for product improvement purposes)

## 3. Why We Collect Your Data (Purposes of Processing)

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

- **Providing the service:** Generating career analyses, tailored CVs, job suggestions, LinkedIn content, and personalized coaching — these are the core functions of Get A Job
- **AI-powered features:** Sending your data to AI models to generate personalized career guidance, content, and recommendations (see Section 5 for details on AI processing)
- **Account management:** Creating and maintaining your account, authenticating your identity, communicating with you about your account
- **Product improvement:** Analyzing how the platform is used to improve features, fix bugs, and develop new functionality. We use aggregated and anonymized usage data wherever possible
- **Security:** Protecting against unauthorized access, fraud, and abuse
- **Legal compliance:** Meeting our obligations under applicable law, including the Israeli Privacy Protection Law

We do **not** use your data for advertising, sell your data to third parties, or use it for purposes unrelated to your career development on our platform.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law (as amended by Amendment 13), we process your data on the following bases:

- **Consent:** You provide informed consent when you create an account and use the platform. Specific features (such as LinkedIn archive upload or AI-powered content generation) involve additional affirmative actions that constitute consent for those processing activities.
- **Legitimate purpose:** Processing is necessary to provide the career guidance services you have requested.
- **Legal obligation:** Where we are required to retain data or respond to lawful requests from authorities.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account (see Section 8). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

## 5. AI Processing and Automated Decision-Making

Get A Job uses artificial intelligence extensively. We want to be transparent about how this works:

### What AI does with your data

When you use features like career analysis, CV generation, LinkedIn content creation, job matching, or chat-based coaching, your relevant personal data is sent to an AI model (provided by OpenAI and Anthropic — see Section 6) to generate personalized outputs. This includes your work experience, education, skills, career goals, and other profile information relevant to the specific feature.

### AI-generated career classifications

Our career analysis feature uses AI to classify your qualification level (e.g., Junior, Mid-level) and recommend career roles organized into tiers (Tier 1: strong fit, Tier 2: stretch, Tier 3: aspirational). These classifications are recommendations intended to guide your job search — they are not binding assessments of your abilities, and you are free to pursue any role regardless of the tier classification.

### Anti-fabrication safeguards

We have built safeguards to prevent AI from inventing information about you. The AI is instructed never to fabricate metrics, company names, accomplishments, or other factual claims. Stories and achievements used in CV generation must be confirmed by you before they are used. However, AI outputs should always be reviewed by you before use — AI can make mistakes.

### Your rights regarding AI processing

You have the right to understand how automated decisions affecting you are made. If you have questions about any AI-generated output or classification, contact us at privacy@getajob.careers.

## 7. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

- **Authentication security:** Email verification, secure password requirements (minimum 8 characters with complexity requirements), protection against leaked passwords (HaveIBeenPwned integration), reauthentication required for sensitive operations
- **Database security:** Row-level security (RLS) on all database tables, ensuring users can only access their own data
- **Access control:** Admin functions are restricted to authorized personnel with defense-in-depth gating
- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our infrastructure providers
- **Security auditing:** We conduct security audits of our platform and have addressed identified vulnerabilities including unauthorized data access paths
- **Rate limiting:** AI-powered features are rate-limited to prevent abuse
- **Observability:** We monitor platform performance and errors to detect and respond to issues quickly

No system is perfectly secure. If you become aware of any security issue, please contact us immediately at privacy@getajob.careers.

## 8. Your Rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law (as amended by Amendment 13), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### Right of access
You may request to see the personal data we hold about you. We will provide this information in an intelligible format in Hebrew or English.

### Right to correction
If any of your personal data is incorrect, incomplete, or outdated, you may request that we correct it. You can also update most of your data directly through the platform's profile editor at any time.

### Right to deletion
You may request the deletion of your personal data. You can:
- **Reset your data** within the platform (clears your profile, career analysis, applications, stories, and generated content while keeping your account active)
- **Delete your account entirely** (removes your account and all associated data from our systems)

Upon receiving a deletion request, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain records.

### Right to object
You may object to the processing of your personal data. If you object to processing that is essential to the service, we may not be able to continue providing the service to you.

### How to exercise your rights
You can exercise these rights by contacting us at privacy@getajob.careers. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and you continue to use the platform.

If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except:
- Aggregated, anonymized data that cannot be linked back to you (retained for product analytics)
- Data we are required to retain by law (e.g., for tax, legal, or regulatory purposes)
- Backup copies, which are purged according to our backup retention schedule

If your account is inactive for an extended period (12 months or more), we may contact you to confirm whether you wish to keep your account. If we receive no response, we may delete your account and associated data after providing reasonable notice.

## 10. Cookies and Tracking

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our platform. We use cookies and similar technologies as follows:

- **Essential cookies:** These are required for the platform to function. They include session authentication tokens (managed by Supabase Auth) that keep you logged in as you navigate between pages. These cookies do not contain your personal data directly — they contain a session identifier that links to your account on our servers. These cookies cannot be disabled without breaking platform functionality.
- **Analytics:** We use product analytics tools (PostHog) to understand how the platform is used, such as which features are most popular and where users encounter issues. These tools may collect your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and session recordings associated with your account. Where possible, this data is anonymized.

We do **not** use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We do **not** serve ads or allow third parties to track you on our platform for advertising purposes.

## 11. Browser Extension

This Privacy Policy also applies to the Get A Job browser extension, an optional companion to your Get A Job account. The extension is designed not to collect any personal data beyond what this policy already describes.

- **Authentication:** The extension works only when you are signed in to getajob.careers in the same browser. It reads your existing Get A Job login session from your open getajob.careers tab in order to authenticate your requests. It does not create a separate login and does not access or store your password.
- **Job descriptions you analyze:** When you explicitly choose to generate a tailored CV or add a role to your application tracker, the extension reads the text of the job posting on the page you are currently viewing, or processes the text you paste into the extension interface. That text is sent to the Get A Job backend (Supabase and the AI providers described in Section 6) for the processing described elsewhere in this policy.
- **Chrome Web Store data-use compliance:** Our use of information received through the extension, including any data accessed via Google APIs, will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
- **What it does not do:** The extension does not automatically monitor, collect, or transmit your browsing history, any page content other than the specific job description you explicitly choose to evaluate, or any data unrelated to the core functions of Get A Job. It does not track you across the web, does not sell your data, and does not serve advertising.
- **Storage and permissions:** The extension uses your browser's local storage only to hold your Get A Job session so it can authenticate. The permissions it requests — access to your getajob.careers tab and to the Get A Job backend — exist solely for the functions described above.
