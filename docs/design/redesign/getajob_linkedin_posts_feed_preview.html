Three months into owning onboarding, I learned the fastest way to cut churn wasn't a new feature — it was a two-line setup checklist.

We'd been blaming the product. The data said otherwise: the users who finished setup in week one stayed. So we made finishing unavoidable, and that cohort's churn dropped.

The lesson I keep coming back to: the problem usually isn't the thing everyone's pointing at.
