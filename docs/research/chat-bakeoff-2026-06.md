## Caveats & confounds

- **CHAT-13 (application action) is confounded:** the fixture says "I just applied to the PM role at Workiz, add it as applied," but Workiz PM is ALREADY in Eli's tracker (status `applied`). The correct behavior is to NOT duplicate-add — so a model declining to fire `add_application` here is arguably right, not wrong. Read CHAT-13's action column as "does the model avoid a duplicate add," not "can it add an application." A clean add-application probe needs a company not already tracked.
- **Action emission is the model's judgment call, by design.** Several fixtures (CHAT-02 tasks, CHAT-04 roadmap, CHAT-12 company-target, CHAT-15 nav) ask for an action but the prompt rules also tell the model to OMIT the block when it isn't warranted (e.g. don't duplicate existing tasks). Divergence across candidates here is signal about how eagerly each model takes structured actions — not a pure pass/fail of capability. Read it alongside the prose.
- **Judge vendor affinity:** the judge (Claude Opus) shares a vendor with the Sonnet candidate. Sonnet's prose scores (advice/voice/grounding) may carry mild upward bias; weight the programmatic action + number-grounding checks more heavily for Sonnet, and trust gpt-4o-mini-vs-gpt-4o prose comparisons most (both cross-vendor to the judge).
- **Temperature 0.4 → non-determinism:** a re-run will shift individual cells. Treat per-cell results as one sample; lean on the aggregate pattern.

### Call: flag-gate a swap to `claude-sonnet-4.6`; rule out `gpt-4o`.

**Why Sonnet.** It sweeps every axis that matters:

- **Quality:** highest advice (3.87 vs 2.87 incumbent), voice (4.00 vs 3.47), grounding (3.73 vs 3.40), and the best action-correctness (12/15 vs 11/15).
- **Latency:** _fastest_ of all three at p50 1503ms — ~35% quicker than the incumbent's 2325ms. This matters specifically because PR-B1 (the drawer) is about to make chat the most-used surface; lower perceived latency is the lift users feel most, and the 2026-05-26 concurrency lesson notes faster replies also raise instantaneous throughput — a win on the surface about to carry the most traffic.
- **Safety:** passes the adversarial anti-fabrication gate (CHAT-14 refused=true), same as both OpenAI models.

**Why not gpt-4o.** It is _dominated_: only marginally above the incumbent on quality (advice 3.07 vs 2.87) while costing ~17× more per turn. Sonnet beats it on every quality axis AND on latency; gpt-4o-mini beats it on cost. It owns no niche — drop it from contention.

**Cost.** Sonnet is **+$379/mo** over the incumbent at the stated 40 turns/user/week × 100 students (~$395/mo absolute, ~25× incumbent cost/turn; ~$296/mo at 30/wk, ~$494/mo at 50/wk). For a 100-student pilot this is a modest line item, and it buys the quality + latency lift on the soon-to-be-busiest surface. Sonnet's per-turn cost is the lever to revisit if cost sensitivity rises beyond the pilot.

**Robustness of the verdict.** The judge shares a vendor with Sonnet (Opus), so discount its prose scores somewhat — but Sonnet _also_ wins the two vendor-neutral, fully-programmatic measures: action-correctness (12/15) and latency (1503ms). The verdict does not rest on judge prose alone. Temperature 0.4 makes any single run one sample, but the action + latency gaps are wide enough to survive re-sampling.

### Before the swap PR (gates, mirroring `cv_model`):

1. **Ship as its own flag-gated PR** — a `chat_model` flag on the `chat-agent` route (`_shared/model-routing.ts`), defaulted to gpt-4o-mini, opt-in for the pilot cohort. The `chat-agent` route placeholder already exists; the PR wires the conversational path to consult `routeFor('chat-agent')` and adds the OpenRouter transport branch in `ai-chat/index.ts`.

Still, run a `validate-cv-deploy`-style live pass against the deployed function on the Sonnet flag before ramp, confirming the frontend `ChatInterface` + action handlers consume Sonnet's real output cleanly (Sonnet fences JSON; verify the live edge path matches the harness).
3. **Truncation/retry audit:** Sonnet ran longer on some action cells; confirm the 2048→4096 retry budget + the OpenRouter retry-parity wrapper hold under the drawer's concurrency before 100% ramp.
