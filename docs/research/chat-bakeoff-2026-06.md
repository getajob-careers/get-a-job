> **Reasoning-model contract:** gpt-5.4 and gpt-5.4-mini reject `max_tokens`. A real chat-route swap to a reasoning model would adopt exactly this shape; with effort='none' a low completion cap can still truncate (watch `finishReason`/err counts below).

Prose scores (advice/voice/grounding) may carry mild upward bias for Sonnet; the programmatic action-correctness + number-grounding checks are vendor-neutral. The three OpenAI candidates are all cross-vendor to the judge.

## Caveats & confounds

- **CHAT-13 (application action) is confounded:** the fixture says "I just applied to the PM role at Workiz, add it as applied," but Workiz PM is ALREADY in Eli's tracker (status `applied`). The correct behavior is to NOT duplicate-add — so a model declining to fire `add_application` here is arguably right, not wrong. Read CHAT-13's action column as "does the model avoid a duplicate add," not "can it add an application." A clean add-application probe needs a company not already tracked.
- **Action emission is the model's judgment call, by design.** Several fixtures (CHAT-02 tasks, CHAT-04 roadmap, CHAT-12 company-target, CHAT-15 nav) ask for an action but the prompt rules also tell the model to OMIT the block when it isn't warranted (e.g. don't duplicate existing tasks). Divergence across candidates here is signal about how eagerly each model takes structured actions — not a pure pass/fail of capability. Read it alongside the prose.
- **Judge vendor affinity:** the judge (Claude Opus) shares a vendor with the Sonnet candidate. Sonnet's prose scores (advice/voice/grounding) may carry mild upward bias; weight the programmatic action + number-grounding checks more heavily for Sonnet, and trust gpt-4o-mini-vs-gpt-4o prose comparisons most (both cross-vendor to the judge).
- **Supplementary set (CHAT-16..19) grounding = deixis honesty.** A high grounding score there means the model did NOT invent an unseen referent ("this role" with nothing selected, "this page" / "these listings" it can't observe) and did NOT author an inline CV (CHAT-19). A low score means it confabulated a referent or fabricated readiness/track vocabulary for a row it can't see. This is the failure mode that motivated the extension; weight it heavily.
- **CHAT-19 inline-CV gate is heuristic:** action-correct fails if the prose contains ≥3 résumé-structure signals (`**Summary**`/`**Experience**`/`**Education**`/`**Skills**`, `[placeholder]` tokens, a multi-bullet role block). The career_agent cannot emit `SUGGESTED_CV_GENERATION_JSON` (no CV_GENERATION_RULES), so the only differentiator is whether the model fabricates a CV in chat vs. routes/declines.
- **Reasoning candidates ran at `reasoning_effort='none'` on the chat budget (2048→4096 as max_completion_tokens).** If gpt-5.4/-mini show elevated error/empty/truncation counts, that is partly the low completion cap interacting with hidden reasoning — a real consideration for using a reasoning model on the chat route, surfaced rather than hidden.
- **Temperature 0.4 → non-determinism:** a re-run will shift individual cells. Treat per-cell results as one sample; lean on the aggregate pattern.

### Call (revised, not sticky to the prior 3-way conclusion): flag-gate a swap to `claude-sonnet-4.6`. It wins more decisively with two reasoning models added, on the criteria that now matter most.

The extension changed the field but not the winner — and it strengthened the case by adding the deixis-honesty + capability-routing axes that the production smoke exposed.

**Sonnet is the only model that is top-tier on BOTH new criteria AND clean on action discipline, at competitive latency:**

- Deixis honesty (supplementary grounding 4.00/4): on page-deixis (CHAT-17) and list-deixis (CHAT-18) it does NOT invent an off-page/​off-context referent — grounding 4 on both, vs the incumbent's 2.
- Capability routing (CHAT-19): refuses to author an inline CV (PASS), where the incumbent and gpt-4o both fabricate a full résumé in chat (the exact production pathology).
- Best aggregate everywhere: full-set action 16/19 (84%), advice 3.79, voice 4.00, grounding 3.95.
- Latency 2333ms p50 — on par with the incumbent and ~2.6× faster than gpt-5.4. Latency is the lift users feel most on the drawer surface.

**Closest rival — gpt-5.4 — is disqualified by latency, not quality.** It matches Sonnet on deixis grounding (supp 4.00) and also refuses the inline CV, but: p50 **6112ms** (~2.6× Sonnet — unacceptable for the most-used surface), ~as expensive ($0.0204 vs $0.0232), and its one supplementary miss (CHAT-19 ✗) is a _spurious task block_, not an inline CV. It offers no quality win over Sonnet and a large latency loss. Rule out.

**The cost-sensitive question, answered plainly: gpt-5.4-mini is NOT within noise of Sonnet on quality — it is the budget upgrade, not a quality substitute.** Full-set quality-composite gap is 1.68/12 and the supplementary-grounding gap is 1.50/4 (grnd 2.50 vs 4.00) — both material, both in Sonnet's favor, and concentrated exactly on the deixis honesty this extension was built to measure (5.4-mini still confabulates referents on CHAT-17/18, grounding 2). That said, 5.4-mini is a real, cheap upgrade over the incumbent: it passes the inline-CV gate the incumbent fails, lifts action-correctness 68%→79%, and costs only **+$75/mo** (5.5× incumbent) vs Sonnet's **+$386/mo** (24×). **Decision framing:** if deixis honesty on the soon-busiest surface is the priority (it is — that's what triggered this work), Sonnet earns its premium; if budget is the hard constraint, gpt-5.4-mini is the defensible fallback and still clearly beats staying on gpt-4o-mini. gpt-4o is dominated on every axis — drop it.

**Net:** primary = **claude-sonnet-4.6** (flag-gated, +$386/mo at 40 turns/wk × 100 students); documented fallback if cost-constrained = **gpt-5.4-mini** (+$75/mo, weaker deixis honesty — name the tradeoff). Ruled out: gpt-4o (dominated), gpt-5.4 (latency).
